# Design Decisions & Tradeoffs

Please use this document to capture your thinking as you work through the exercise. There are no right or wrong answers here — we want to understand *how* you approach problems, not just the final code.

## Architecture & Design

> How did you structure the application? What layers or abstractions did you create, and why?



## Routing Logic

> How did you implement the routing rules? How easy would it be to add, remove, or reorder rules?



## Data Access

> How does your application interact with the database or data layer? What patterns did you use and why?



## Error Handling

> How do you handle invalid input, missing data, and edge cases? What tradeoffs did you make?



## Testing Strategy

> What did you choose to test, and what did you skip? How did you decide what was worth covering?



## Assumptions

> What ambiguities did you encounter, and what assumptions did you make to resolve them?



## If I Had More Time...

> What would you add, change, or refactor if this were heading to production? Consider things like performance, observability, resiliency, security, and operability.
